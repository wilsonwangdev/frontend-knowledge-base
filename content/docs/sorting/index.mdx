---
title: Sorting
---

Why is sorting important? Sorting is a fundamental operation in computer science that is used to arrange elements in a specific order. 
It is a crucial step in many algorithms and data structures, and it is often used to improve the efficiency of other operations.

Sorting is important because it allows us to:

- Find elements quickly
- Compare elements
- Group similar elements together
